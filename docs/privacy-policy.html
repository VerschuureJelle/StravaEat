Privacy Policy — StravaEat

Last updated: June 7, 2026

StravaEat ("we", "our", or "the app") is a mobile application that connects to Strava to calculate energy expenditure and helps athletes plan their nutrition. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account information: email address, name, date of birth (provided during signup).
  • Health & fitness data: heart rate, calories burned, workout duration and distance — imported from Strava with your explicit permission.
  • Nutrition data: food items and calorie intake you log manually in the app.
  • Body metrics: weight, height, resting and maximum heart rate — entered by you in Settings.
  • Location: approximate location used solely to fetch local weather forecasts. Location is never stored on our servers.
  • Purchase information: subscription status managed by RevenueCat. We do not store payment details.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To calculate personalised calorie targets and nutrition recommendations.
  • To provide AI-powered coaching via the Coach feature (powered by Anthropic Claude).
  • To display weather-based workout recommendations.
  • To manage your subscription and credit balance.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with the following service providers, solely to operate the app:

  • Supabase — secure database and authentication (EU region).
  • Strava — fitness data, with your explicit OAuth consent.
  • Anthropic — AI coaching responses (your message and basic profile data are sent; no data is used to train models).
  • RevenueCat — subscription management.
  • Open-Meteo — weather data (no personal data sent).

4. Data Retention

Your data is stored as long as your account is active. You may request deletion at any time (see section 6).

5. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Strava tokens are stored encrypted. We apply row-level security so users can only access their own data.

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You can request access to or deletion of your data at any time by emailing us at verschuure.jelle@gmail.com. We will process requests within 30 days. You can also delete your account directly from the app via Settings.

7. Children

StravaEat is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. The updated date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance.

9. Contact

Questions? Email us at verschuure.jelle@gmail.com
